title: "Sigfox"
---

To enable Sigfox, perform the steps below:

1. Set the toggle to ON.
2. Select SIGFOX as Source type.
3. Enter the SIGFOX endpoint URL (default is https://sigfox.crystalunivers.com).
4. Fill in the Login, Password, and Installation Reference.<br />
   <Frame>
     ![Sig Fox Conf](/images/Workspace/Administrator/SigFox-conf.png "Sig Fox Conf")
   </Frame>
5. Click Save
