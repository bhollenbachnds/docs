---
title: "Dashboard"
---

The **Dashboard** (`/`) is the landing page for PADS4 users. It gives a live, real-time view of every device's connectivity, fetched from PADS4 on every load. There's no polling on this page, so refresh to get the latest.

- **Summary cards**: total devices, online count, offline count, with percentages.
- **Filters**: free-text search (device name), status (online/offline), group, brand. Filters combine, and results are paginated (15 per page).
- **Table columns**: Name, Status, Online since, Offline since, Offline duration, Group, Brand. Click any column header to sort; click again to reverse.
- **Empty states**: if filters return nothing, the empty-state message offers a **Clear all filters** button directly.

This page always reflects PADS4's current state at the moment you loaded it. It does not use the plugin's local history database.
