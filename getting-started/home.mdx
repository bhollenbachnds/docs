## Switch to another part

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dropdown">
    At the top of the left navigation, select the dropdown that shows the current part of PADS4.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a part">
    Select the part that matches your task, for example **PADS4 Digital Signage**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Not sure which part you need? Use the search at the top of the page. Search looks through all parts at the same time.
</Tip>
