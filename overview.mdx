---
title: "Overview"
---

Signing in as an administrator lands on **Overview** (`/`, admin variant) — a minimal landing page prompting you to open **Settings** to finish configuring PADS4 connectivity, background monitoring, proof of play, and email delivery, then sign out and back in as a PADS4 user to use the reporting features day-to-day. There's no separate "admin dashboard" beyond this and Settings — administration is intentionally kept to configuration, not day-to-day monitoring.
