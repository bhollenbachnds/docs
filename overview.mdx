---
title: "Overview"
---

When you sign in as an administrator, you land on **Overview** (`/`, admin variant). This minimal landing page prompts you to open **Settings** to finish configuring PADS4 connectivity, background monitoring, proof of play, and email delivery. Then sign out and back in as a PADS4 user to use the reporting features day-to-day. There's no separate "admin dashboard" beyond this and Settings. Administration is intentionally kept to configuration, not day-to-day monitoring.
