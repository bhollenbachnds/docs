Solstice is a software-based, cloud-enabled meeting collaboration platform that is optimized for both local and remote teams. It allows simultaneous users to wirelessly share content to the room display and seamlessly bridges to remote locations using any major video conferencing service. With Solstice in your meeting spaces, you get the productivity gains of wireless collaboration with the power of fully integrated video conferencing. For more information, visit the Mersive website: [https://www.mersive.com/](https://www.mersive.com/)

Before you start, get the required keys from the Mersive Knowledge Base.<br />

Enable the slider in the top-right corner to "ON" and fill in the following information:

#### Login information

Mersive SDS API: _Fill in the link here_

Password: _Fill in the password for the SDS API link here_

URL PADS4: _Enter your localhost URL here_

When this information is correct, click the **Save** button. To verify the connection, check that the connection is valid in your Mersive environment.
