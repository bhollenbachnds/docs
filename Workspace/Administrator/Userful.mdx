With Userful, you can empower PADS4 CMS with Userful's Visual Networking Platform. This feature-rich, easy-to-use approach to deploying digital displays and video walls helps you reduce operating costs and rapidly implement new initiatives to achieve your organization’s goals.

To read more about Userful, go to the Userful website: [https://www.userful.com/](https://www.userful.com/)

Enable the slider in the top-right corner to "ON" and fill in the following information:

### Login information

Provide the following information to connect with your Userful account:

Userful API: _Provide the link from your Userful connection._

Username: _For example: [user@pads4.com](mailto:user@pads4.com)_

Password: _Your password_
