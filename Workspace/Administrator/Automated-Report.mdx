---
title: "Automated reports"
description: "Automatically generate PADS4 CMS reports for administrators and stakeholders by setting email addresses, export type, granularity, and report frequency."
---

Within the PADS4 CMS modules, you can automatically generate reports for your administrators or other stakeholders within certain timeframes.

<Note>
  You need to configure your SMTP server to send out automated reports.
</Note>

Enable the slider in the top-right corner to "ON" and fill in the following information:

#### Configuration

**E-mail addresses**: _Add email addresses here. You can add multiple users._

**Export type**: _Select one or more file types to export: PDF (.pdf), Excel (.xlsx), or both._

**Granularity**: _Select the scope of your report_. _Choose from: All buildings / By location / Per building_

**Frequency**: Select the frequency of the automated reports from: Weekly / Monthly / Quarterly / Bi-annually / Annually

Once you have selected all these properties, click the **Save** button. The automated reports are sent out from the moment you save.
