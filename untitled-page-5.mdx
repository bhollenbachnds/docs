title: "Proof of Play"
---

**Proof of Play** (`/proof-of-play`) answers "what played, where, and how much" — built from PADS4's play-log data, synced continuously into the plugin's local database in the background (not fetched live per page view, which is what makes this page fast).

If PADS4 isn't configured yet, this page shows a plain message asking an administrator to complete Settings instead of any data.

### Banners you might see

- **Collecting proof-of-play history — X of Y days synced.** Appears while the background sync is still backfilling history after first setup (or after retention settings change). It updates itself automatically every 10 seconds — data appears in the tables as each day finishes syncing, no need to reload the page.
- **"The last sync from PADS4 failed. Showing the most recently collected data."** The background sync hit an error on its most recent attempt; you're looking at the last successful data, not a live failure of the page itself.

### Filters

Date range (both dates inclusive), an optional time-of-day window (From/To hour), Device, Element, and a free-text Search box that matches content, element, or device name (debounced — it waits for you to stop typing before querying).

### The three views

1. **Summary** — grouped by device \+ element \+ content: play count, total time on screen, average completion, first/last play. Sort by Plays or Duration.
2. **By media** — grouped by content title first (which is usually what you actually want to know: "how many times did _this_ run, and where"). Expand a row to see its per-device breakdown. Sort by Plays, Duration, or number of Devices.
3. **Event log** — the raw play-by-play list: every individual play event with start time (plus UTC offset if non-zero), device, element, content, duration, whether it played to completion, and whether the device's monitor was reported on.

A **Plays per day** bar chart sits above the tables, reflecting your current filters.

**Create report from this view**: the button in the page header jumps to Reports with your current date range, device/element filters, and search term carried over, ready to export.
