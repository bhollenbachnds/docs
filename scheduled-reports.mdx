---
title: "Scheduled reports"
description: "Set up scheduled reports that email Connectivity, Offline devices, or Device distribution PDFs daily, weekly, or monthly, and manage active schedules."
---

**Scheduled reports** (`/scheduled`) emails a PDF on a recurring cadence. You can schedule Connectivity, Offline devices, or Device distribution reports. Proof of play reports aren't schedulable.

You must configure SMTP in **Settings** first. If SMTP isn't configured, a banner tells you so, and schedules won't send until you configure it.

1. Click **Add schedule**.
2. Select **Report type** and **Cadence** (Daily / Weekly / Monthly).
3. Enter one or more **Recipients** as comma- or semicolon-separated email addresses. The app validates the addresses before you can submit.
4. Set the **Device scope** using the same group-tree/brand/device panel described in [Device scope](/reports#device-scope).
5. Click **Create schedule**.

The **Active schedules** table lists every configured schedule. Each row includes:

- A scope summary column.
- An **Enabled/Disabled** toggle, which pauses delivery without deleting the schedule.
- A **Delete** action, which opens a confirmation dialog. You can't undo a deletion.

Schedule changes take effect immediately. You don't need to restart the server.
