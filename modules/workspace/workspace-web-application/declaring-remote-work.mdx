In the PADS4 Workspace app, you can **declare remote work days** (also known as telework). Your administrator must first turn on this option in the back office.

Your administrator can assign you a number of remote work days per year. You manage and declare these days in the app.

---

### **Step 1: Open the remote work tool**

In your calendar, select the **blue icon** at the bottom right of the screen.

---

### **Step 2: Select your remote work period**

- Select the **date or range** of days for which you plan to work remotely.  
- The screen shows the number of selected **half-days**.  
- The bottom left of the screen shows your remaining **annual remote work days**.

---

### **Step 3: Save your remote work declaration**

Select **Save** to confirm your remote work declaration.

---

### **Step 4: View the entry in your calendar**

After you save, the remote work entry appears in **blue** in your calendar and marks the period that you declared.
