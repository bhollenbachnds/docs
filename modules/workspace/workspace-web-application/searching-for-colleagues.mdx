PADS4 Workspace lets you **search for colleagues** to see where they are scheduled to work. This feature is available if your administrator turns it on.

Use it to find co-workers in a shared office or to plan meetings and desk use.

---

### Step 1: Open the search tool

From the **main menu**, select **Search for Colleagues**.

---

### Step 2: Select a colleague and time period

1. Use the **directory** on the left to find and select a colleague.  
2. Select a **time period** to view their scheduled workspace reservations and availability.

This view shows you where the selected person will be and helps you find the best time and place to work together.
