The Workspace web application is the browser version of PADS4 Workspace for users. You use it to find a space, check when it is free, and book it. You also use it to manage your own bookings, remote work days, and absences in one calendar.

To open the application, go to the Workspace address of your organization and sign in with your account. 

## What you can do

- **Find a space**: Browse rooms and desks in a list, a grid, or on an interactive floor plan. Filter by name, capacity, space type, location, equipment, and services. See [Search for a space](/modules/workspace/workspace-web-application/searching-for-a-space).
- **Book a space**: Reserve a room or desk, invite participants and external visitors, set reminders, and add services such as catering. See [Booking a space](/modules/workspace/workspace-web-application/booking-a-space).
- **Book a common space or a group workspace**: See [Booking a common space](/modules/workspace/workspace-web-application/booking-a-common-space) and [Booking a group workspace](/modules/workspace/workspace-web-application/booking-a-group-workspace).
- **Save favorites**: Mark the rooms and desks that you use most, so they appear at the top of your list. See [Favourite Room](/modules/workspace/workspace-web-application/FavouriteRoom).
- **Manage your calendar**: See your bookings, remote work days, and absences. See [My calendar](/modules/workspace/workspace-web-application/my-calendar).
- **Declare remote work and report absence**: See [Declaring remote work](/modules/workspace/workspace-web-application/declaring-remote-work) and [Reporting absence](/modules/workspace/workspace-web-application/reporting-absence).
- **Find colleagues**: See [Searching for colleagues](/modules/workspace/workspace-web-application/searching-for-colleagues).

## Which spaces you can see and book

Your administrator decides which spaces you can see and book. By default, users can view and book all available spaces. An administrator can limit your access to selected spaces, or prevent you from booking.

Some rooms need extra steps:

- **Approval**: A booking for some rooms stays _pending_ until an approver confirms it. See [Workflows](/modules/workspace/workspace-web-application/Workflows).
- **Booking request**: In some configurations, you describe what you need, and a manager assigns a room. See [Workflows](/modules/workspace/workspace-web-application/Workflows).
- **Modular rooms and delegation**: See [Special cases](/modules/workspace/workspace-web-application/special-cases).

## Other ways to book

You can also book spaces in the [Workspace mobile application](/modules/workspace/workspace-mobile-application/introduction) and, if your administrator turned it on, in the Outlook add-in.
