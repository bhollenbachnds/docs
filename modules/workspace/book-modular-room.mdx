---
title: "Book a modular room"
description: "Book one part of a divisible room or the whole combined room in PADS4 Workspace, for example a training area or a boardroom with movable partitions."
---

A modular room is a room that you can combine or separate with movable partitions. Examples are a flexible training area or a boardroom that can be resized. In PADS4 Workspace, you book one of the separate rooms, or the combined larger room.

## Before you begin

Your administrator must set up the modular space. If you cannot find a combined room, ask your administrator. See [Modular spaces](/modules/workspace/workspace-admin/modular-spaced).

## Book a modular room

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the room">
    Search for the room. See [Search for a space](/modules/workspace/search-for-a-space).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the room or the combined room">
    Select one of the individual rooms, or select the combined room.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the booking">
    Select the **\+** button, enter the details, and save the booking. See [Book a space](/modules/workspace/book-a-space).
  </Step>
</Steps>
