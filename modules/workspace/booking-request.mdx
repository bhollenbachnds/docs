---
title: "Submit a booking request"
description: "Describe what you need in PADS4 Workspace, such as date, building, and equipment, and let a manager assign a suitable room to your request."
---

In some configurations, you do not select a room yourself. Instead, you submit a _booking request_ with your criteria. A manager reviews the request and assigns a suitable room.

## Roles

| Role | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Requester** | The person who submits the request with criteria. |
| **Manager** | The person who reviews requests and assigns rooms. |

## Before you begin

Your administrator must allow you to send booking requests to your manager. See [Users](/modules/workspace/workspace-admin/users). The administrator also assigns the manager.

## Submit a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the request">
    In your calendar, select **Reservation Request**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your criteria">
    Enter what you need, for example the date, the building, the equipment, and the services.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the request">
    Save the request. The request appears in yellow in your calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the room">
    You receive an email that says your request is awaiting assignment and approval. When the manager assigns a room, you receive a confirmation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Assign a room to a request

If you are a manager, you receive a notification when a request is waiting.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the request">
    In PADS4 Workspace, open the pending reservation request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the request">
    Accept the request. A list of rooms that match the criteria opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a room">
    Select a room from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finalize the booking">
    Select **Save**. The requester receives a confirmation that a room is assigned.
  </Step>
</Steps>
