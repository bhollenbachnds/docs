description: "Learn about the booking options in PADS4 Workspace that go beyond a standard booking: modular rooms, approval, booking requests, and delegation."
---

Most bookings are simple: you select a space and save. Your administrator can set up extra options for some spaces and users. Use this page to find out what each option does and where to read more.

## Options at a glance

| Option | What it does | Who uses it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Modular rooms** | Lets you book a single room, or the combined room when partitions are open. | Users |
| **Approval** | Holds a booking as _pending_ until an approver confirms it. | Users and approvers |
| **Booking request** | Lets you describe what you need, so that a manager assigns a room. | Users and managers |
| **Delegation** | Lets one user manage bookings for another user. | Delegates, for example assistants |

Your administrator decides which options apply to you. If you do not see an option, ask your administrator.
