This guide shows you how to install and configure the **PADS4 Workspace** mobile application on **Apple iOS** and **Android** devices.

---

## Prerequisites

Before you install the application, make sure you have:

- A valid **Apple ID** (for iOS) or **Google account** (for Android) signed in on the device  
- An active **single sign-on (SSO)** account  
- **Internet** or **local network** connectivity through Wi-Fi or mobile data  

---

## Install the application

To install the **PADS4 Workspace** application:

1. Open the **App Store** (iOS) or **Google Play** (Android).  
2. Search for:  
   ```text
   PADS4 Workspace
   ```
3. Tap the application, then tap **Download** or **Install**.

---

## Connect to PADS4 Workspace

After you install the application, connect it to the PADS4 Workspace environment of your organization:

1. Open the **PADS4 Workspace** application.
2. Enter the PADS4 instance URL of your organization. For example:  
   ```text
   https://yourservername.domain.com
   ```
3. Tap **Save**.

---

## Sign in with SSO

To access your account:

1. On the sign-in screen, tap **Single Sign-On (SSO)**.
2. Enter your SSO credentials, such as your company email address and password.

After PADS4 Workspace authenticates you, you are signed in and ready to use the application.

---

## Workspace mobile interface

After you sign in, the application shows the PADS4 Workspace interface. Use it to:

- Search and book available rooms
- View your reservations
- Receive notifications and more
