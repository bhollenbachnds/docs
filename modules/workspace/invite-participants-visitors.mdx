---
title: "Invite participants and visitors"
description: "Add colleagues and external visitors to a booking in PADS4 Workspace, so they receive an invitation and, for visitors, a QR code for check-in."
---

Add people to a booking so that they receive an invitation. You can invite colleagues as participants and guests from outside your organization as external visitors.

## Add participants

1. In the booking window, go to **Participants**.
2. Select a person from your contact list, or enter the person manually.
3. Select **Save** to save the booking.

Each participant receives an email that tells them about the invitation.

## Add external visitors

External visitors are guests from outside your organization. This function uses a Visitor Management System (VMS).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a visitor">
    In the booking window, go to **External Visitors**, and then select the **\+** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the visitor details">
    Enter the required information for the visitor. 
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the visitor">
    Select **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the booking">
    Select **Save** in the booking window.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Participants and capacity

For a common space, participants count toward the number of available spots. See [Book a common space](/modules/workspace/workspace-web-application/booking-a-common-space).
