The **Home** tab in the Workspace Administration section provides administrators with a comprehensive snapshot of the current status of the PADS4 Workspace module, along with valuable usage statistics and operational insights.

## Available Metrics & Insights

- **Defective Equipment**<br />View all reported issues related to defective equipment within the workspace environment.
- **Room Occupancy**<br />Displays the real-time status of rooms and spaces—whether they are free or booked—within the PADS4 Workspace module.
- **Ghost Meetings**<br />Identifies meetings that were reserved but had no check-in, helping to reduce wasted space and improve room usage.
- **Occupancy Rate**<br />Shows the overall occupancy percentage, allowing for data-driven space management decisions.
