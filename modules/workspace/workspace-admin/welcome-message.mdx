## Updating the home page

To personalize the home page:

1. Select **Add an Image** to upload or select a new logo.
2. Enter a custom **welcome message** in the provided field.
3. After you make all changes, click **Save** to apply them.

These updates appear on the PADS4 Workspace user-facing home screen.
