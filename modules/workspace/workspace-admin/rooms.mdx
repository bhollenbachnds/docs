---
title: "Rooms"
---

When you enter the Rooms tab, you will see the following modules:

**Rooms**: these are the standard rooms that you create/import. These rooms are defined by a room name, type, email address,

**Modular spaces**: these are multi-disciplinary rooms that can be combined during events. An invisible group if you will.

**Common rooms**: You can define common areas such as cafeteria areas, entrances or anything in regards to the specific building.
