The PADS4 Workspace mobile application brings PADS4 Workspace to your phone or tablet. Use it to find and book a space, check your reservations, and receive notifications when you are not at your desk.

## What you can do

- **Search and book spaces**: Find an available room or desk, and book it.
- **View your reservations**: See what you booked.
- **Receive notifications**: Get notified about your bookings.

## What you need

| Requirement | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Device** | A phone or tablet with Apple iOS or Android. |
| **Store account** | An Apple ID on iOS, or a Google account on Android, to download the application. |
| **Sign-in account** | A PADS4 account, or a single sign-on (SSO) account of your organization. |
| **Connection** | An internet connection or a connection to your local network, through Wi-Fi or mobile data. |
| **Server address** | The address of the PADS4 Workspace server of your organization. Your administrator gives you this. |
