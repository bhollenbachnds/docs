---
title: "Turn on colleague search"
description: "Turn on the Search for colleague feature in the PADS4 Workspace privacy settings, so that users can see where their colleagues are scheduled to work."
---

Colleague search lets users see where their colleagues are scheduled to work. The feature is off until you turn it on in the privacy settings of PADS4 Workspace.

## Before you begin

Colleague search shows reservations of users to other users. Check that this fits the privacy rules of your organization before you turn it on.

## Turn on colleague search

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the application settings">
    In the admin interface, open the Workspace application settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the privacy settings">
    Go to the privacy settings. These settings apply to all bookings in PADS4 Workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the feature">
    Turn on **Search for colleague**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the settings">
    Save your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The same privacy settings control whether the name of the organizer is visible for bookings.

## Verify the setting

Ask a user to check that **Search for Colleagues** is in the main menu of PADS4 Workspace. See [Search for colleagues](/modules/workspace/search-for-colleagues).
