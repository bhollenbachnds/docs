---
title: "Booking approvals and requests"
description: "Learn how bookings that need approval and booking requests work in PADS4 Workspace, who does what, and which emails and statuses you see."
---

Some bookings in PADS4 Workspace are not final when you save them. A person must first approve the booking, or assign a room to it. PADS4 Workspace guides everyone in the process with emails and with the status of the entry in the calendar.

## Two processes

|  | Booking that needs approval | Booking request |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **When it applies** | You select a specific room, and the room needs approval. | You cannot select a room. You describe what you need. |
| **What you do** | Book the room as usual. | Submit your criteria, such as date, building, equipment, and services. |
| **Who decides** | The approver confirms the booking. | The manager assigns a room, and confirms the booking. |
| **Status in your calendar** | **Pending** | Yellow entry |
| **Email to you at the start** | Your booking is awaiting approval. | Your request is awaiting assignment and approval. |
| **Email to you at the end** | The booking is approved. | A room is assigned. |

## Roles

| Role | Description | Read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Requester** | Books a room or submits a request. | [Book a room that needs approval](/modules/workspace/book-with-approval), [Submit a booking request](/modules/workspace/booking-request) |
| **Approver** | Approves bookings for rooms that need approval. | [Approve a booking](/modules/workspace/book-with-approval#approve-a-booking) |
| **Manager** | Reviews booking requests and assigns rooms. | [Assign a room to a booking request](/modules/workspace/booking-request#assign-a-room-to-a-request) |
| **Administrator** | Turns on approval, allows booking requests, and sets who approves or manages. | [Set up approvals and booking requests](/modules/workspace/booking-approval) |
