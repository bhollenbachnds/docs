Install the application once on your Apple device. After the installation, connect it to the PADS4 Workspace environment of your organization.

## Before you begin

- Sign in on your device with an Apple ID.
- Check that your device has an internet connection, or a connection to your local network, through Wi-Fi or mobile data.
- Make sure that you have an account: a PADS4 account, or an SSO account. See [Sign in to the mobile application](/modules/workspace/sign-in).

## Install the application

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the App Store">
    On your device, open the **App Store**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for the application">
    Search for `PADS4 Workspace`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the application">
    Select the application, and then tap **Download**. 
  </Step>
</Steps>
