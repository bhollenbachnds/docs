With colleague search, you see where a colleague is scheduled to work. Use it to find co-workers in a shared office, to choose the best time and place for a meeting, or to plan which desk to use.

## How colleague search works

- You select a colleague in a directory, and then select a time period.
- PADS4 Workspace shows the workspace reservations and the availability of that colleague in the period.
- You use this to decide when and where to meet.

## What decides whether you can use it

Colleague search is available only if your administrator turned it on. If you do not see **Search for Colleagues** in the main menu, ask your administrator. See [Turn on colleague search](/modules/workspace/admin/turn-on-colleague-search).

Your administrator also controls whether the name of the organizer of a booking is visible.
