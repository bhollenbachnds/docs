Sign in with the same account that you use for PADS4 Workspace in the browser. You can use single sign-on (SSO), or a PADS4 account.

## Before you begin

Install the application on [iOS](/modules/workspace/mobile-install-ios) or [Android](/modules/workspace/mobile-install-android), and [connect it to your organization](/modules/workspace/mobile-connect). You also need an account: an SSO account of your organization, or a PADS4 account.

## Sign in

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="PADS4 account">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Enter your account details">
        On the sign-in screen, enter the user name and the password of your PADS4 account.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign in">
        Tap the sign-in button.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Single sign-on">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select single sign-on">
        On the sign-in screen, tap **Single Sign-On (SSO)**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter your credentials">
        Enter the credentials of your SSO account, for example your company email address and your password.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Check>
  The PADS4 Workspace interface opens. You can now search and book spaces, and view your reservations.
</Check>
