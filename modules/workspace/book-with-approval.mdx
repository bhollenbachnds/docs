Some rooms need approval. You book the room as usual, but the booking is not final until an approver confirms it. Until then, the booking has the status _pending_.

## Roles

| Role | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Requester** | The person who books the room. |
| **Approver** | The person who is responsible for approving bookings of the room. |

## Before you begin

Your administrator turns on approval for a room, and decides who approves. See [Rooms](/modules/workspace/workspace-admin/adding-room).

## Book a room that needs approval

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the booking">
    Select the room, and then select the **\+** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details and save">
    Enter the booking details, and select **Save**. See [Book a space](/modules/workspace/book-a-space).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the approval">
    You receive an email that says your booking is awaiting approval. The booking appears as **Pending** in your calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive the result">
    When the approver confirms the booking, you receive an email that says the booking is approved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Approve a booking

If you are an approver, you receive an email when someone requests a booking. Review the request, and validate it to confirm the meeting.
