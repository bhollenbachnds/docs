#### Dashboard

Welcome to the PADS4 CMS 1.0 dashboard. The digital signage section of PADS4 CMS 1.0 allows users to upload content, create messages and schedule this content to certain PADS4 presentations and screens. After setting up your Administrator section with users, you can start by setting up your first forms, messages and more.

Here is an overview what you can see on the dashboard when you login for the first time with your Administrator account:

• A welcome message in the top left.<br />• A scrolling news feed example text in the top right.<br />• A broadcast messages module that showcases your current/past scheduled messages.<br />• A My History module that showcases your previous created messages.

On the left-hand side you will find the following tabs:

• **Home**: This will take you to the home screen / dashboard of the CMS section.<br />• **Category**: Here you can setup forms/categories/messages for PADS4 CMS users.<br />• **Broadcast**: Here you can view your currently broadcasted messages and their schedule.<br />• **History**: Here you can view the history of created messages.<br />• **Screens**: Here you can setup & manage all your PADS4 CMS screens.\*<br />• **Newsfeed**: Here you can setup a message for your users within PADS4 CMS.

Important: Screens do not sync automatically with your PADS4 installation (Viewers setup in System Management, CTRL Center or Agent). You have to manually configure and setup the screens with the same name (no special characters) inside PADS4 CMS - Digital Signage - Screens to implement the functionality correctly.
