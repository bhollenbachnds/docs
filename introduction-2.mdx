---
title: "PADS4 Canva integration overview"
description: "Introduction to PADS4's native Canva integration that lets designers access Canva designs directly inside digital signage presentations."
---

PADS4 includes a native integration with Canva, so you can access and use Canva designs directly in your digital signage presentations. With this integration, you can use Canva’s extensive design capabilities while keeping content management and scheduling centralized in PADS4.

To use this functionality, you must establish a connection between Canva and PADS4 using a secure authentication process. Once connected, you can browse, select, and update Canva designs directly from within PADS4 Design, streamlining the content creation workflow.
