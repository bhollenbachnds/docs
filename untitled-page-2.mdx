---
title: "Distribution"
---

**Distribution** (`/distribution`) shows how your device estate breaks down by **brand** and by **group** — counts and percentage share for each.

- Loads from a cache first if you have one from a previous visit (stored in your browser), then refreshes from PADS4 in the background — you'll see a **Refreshing…** banner while that happens, and the page still shows the last-known data if the refresh fails.
- Click **Refresh** to force an update.
- Two tables: **By brand** and **By group**, each with Count and Share columns.
